## 2. Plan Identity Card Specifications

Each card is rendered with a dark, premium glassmorphism background (using tokens from our v1.0 spec), structured typography, and prominent feature groupings.

---

### Tier 1: Free
- **Card Title:** **FREE**
- **Brand Identity Statement:** *"Explore Market Intelligence"*
- **Monthly Investment:** **$0**
- **Target Audience:** Market novices, retail students, and unauthenticated guests testing raw data feeds.
- **Included Capabilities:**
  - Standard multi-market price visualizations.
  - Base horizon trend indicators (Short-horizon).
  - Standard timezone-normalized chronological candle streams.
- **Technical Limits:**
  - Maximum 3 concurrent active symbols.
  - Short-horizon analysis only (M1, M5, M15).
  - Basic Overnight Research Reports (read-only PDF).
  - No access to AI Assistant chatbot, backtests, or simulated shadow trading.
- **Card CTA:** `[ Access Basic Terminal ]`
  - *Context microcopy:* "Instant guest login. No credential entry or payment required."
