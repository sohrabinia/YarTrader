# RG_V3 Research Framework

The Research Intelligence Framework converts raw normalized time-series data into structured historical observations and qualitative market insights.

---

## 1. Research Framework Mission

The core mission of the Research Framework is to:
* **Perform Deep Structural Analysis:** Translate plain bar series into multi-dimensional market observation blocks.
* **Track Historical Findings:** Maintain a local registry of past research outputs (`ResearchHistory`) and statistical coefficients (`ResearchMetrics`).
* **Shield Downstream Strategy Layers:** Encapsulate indicators as parameter-driven definitions (`IndicatorDefinition`), keeping them strictly descriptive.

---

## 2. Dependencies and Direction

* **Dependencies:** Core Entities, Data Abstractions.
* **Core Rule:** Core remains completely independent of Research, and Research contains zero concepts of portfolio risk, asset allocation weights, or broker execution.
