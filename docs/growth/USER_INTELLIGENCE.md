# TradeYar AI User Behavioral Intelligence & Segmentation

To personalize SaaS terminal UI views, reader interactions are tracked and segmented dynamically.

## Telemetry Profiling Parameters

- **Articles Read Count**: Increments whenever a visitor accesses a long-form research post.
- **Shadow Trades Watched**: Records count of virtual trade detail clicks on the dashboard.
- **Active Duration**: Cumulative session duration.

## Dynamic User Segments
- **Research User**: Active readers who frequently interact with both deep research articles and shadow trade details.
- **Professional**: Heavy traders focusing primarily on live technical positions.
- **Intermediate**: Standard traders exploring platform signals.
- **Beginner**: New users with low active watch times.
- **Investor**: High net-worth profiles seeking macro briefs.
