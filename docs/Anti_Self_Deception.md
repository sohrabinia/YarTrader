# Anti Self-Deception Layer

## 1. Overview
The **Anti Self-Deception Layer** prevents common cognitive fallacies from corrupting the self-learning market discovery system.

---

## 2. Protected Vulnerabilities
- **Look-Ahead & Future Leakage**: Restricts any data accessing future timestamps during historical replays.
- **Cherry-Picking & History Editing**: Stored virtual trades are immutable. Failures are preserved as required learning materials.
- **Overfitting & Snooping**: Monitored by the Quality Control Brain and the Independent Judge.
