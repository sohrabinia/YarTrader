# Independent Judge Brain

## 1. Overview
To prevent self-deception and bias, the **Independent Judge Brain** acts as an isolated evaluator.

---

## 2. Evaluation Boundaries
The Judge:
- Receives: Simulated Decisions, supporting evidence, and actual out-of-sample outcomes.
- Evaluates: Reasoning Quality, sample sufficiency, consistency, and scientific validity.
- Cannot: Modify history, trade real capital, or update pattern confidence directly.
- Standard: A winning decision can still be judged as bad if the underlying reasoning was weak.
