# YarTrader V1.2 Trading Curriculum & Operating Principles

## Core Trading Philosophy
YarTrader operates under four fundamental trading principles:
1. **Probability Over Prediction:** Markets are probabilistic environments. Trade decisions are framed by empirical statistical edge, not prediction.
2. **Risk Management Over Frequency:** Capital preservation is paramount. The system prioritizes high-quality setups with asymmetric risk/reward.
3. **Quality Over Quantity:** Irrelevant or noisy market setups are filtered out.
4. **Waiting is a Valid Decision:** Staying flat in cash during uncertain, illiquid, or high-spread market conditions is an active professional choice (`WAIT`).

---

## Price Action & Market Structure Framework
YarTrader strictly refrains from technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, Bollinger Bands). All market perception is built on pure Price Action:

### 1. Structure Classification
- **Higher Highs (HH) & Higher Lows (HL):** Bullish trend continuation.
- **Lower Highs (LH) & Lower Lows (LL):** Bearish trend continuation.
- **Equal Highs / Lows:** Compression & Range-bound fair value pricing.

### 2. Liquidity & Traps
- **Liquidity Sweep:** Price sweeps above key resistance or below key support to capture stop orders before reversing back into value range.
- **Market Structure Shift (ChoCh):** Invalidation of local swing high/low structure marking potential trend reversal.

## Timeframe Policy
- **Official Trading Timeframes:** `M1`, `M5`, `M15`, `H1`, `H4`, `D1`, `W1`.
- **Microstructure Research (Non-Trading):** `Tick` resolution is reserved strictly for offline Market Microstructure Research and execution slippage simulation.
