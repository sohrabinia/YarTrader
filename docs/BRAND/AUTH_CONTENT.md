## 4. Multi-Step Onboarding Journey (The Real-World Narrative Shift)

*To prevent the common retail expectation of entering a "signal room" or "copy-trading pool," the onboarding wizard enforces a professional, educational, and analytical tone. Each step requires explicit interaction and confirmation.*

---

### Step 1: The Principle of Zero-Execution Compliance
- **Visual Asset:** An illustration of a locked gate showing data flowing in, but zero commands flowing out.
- **Screen Title:** **Principle 1: Absolute Read-Only Architecture**
- **Copy:**
  Welcome to your TradeYar AI workspace. Before proceeding, you must understand that **this is not a trading execution bot**. Under strict compliance with APES-FIN standards, TradeYar AI is engineered with absolute read-only permissions.

  The system captures live market rates, extracts patterns, and outputs passive reasoning. It is permanently blocked from connecting to your live funded broker accounts or executing real trades on your behalf. You are the ultimate executor.
- **Step Button:** `[ I Understand & Choose Complete Capital Control ]`

---

### Step 2: Escaping the Lagging Indicator Trap
- **Visual Asset:** A side-by-side comparison. Left: A messy retail chart with MACD, RSI, and EMAs overlapping and lagging. Right: A clean, high-fidelity black-and-gold chart highlighting compression zones, expansion ranges, and reaction nodes.
- **Screen Title:** **Principle 2: Pure Non-Linear Price Action**
- **Copy:**
  TradeYar AI does not use RSI, MACD, or moving averages. These lagging indicator algorithms only summarize past price action, creating a dangerous confirmation bias.

  Your workspace is powered by our **Cognitive Engine**, which evaluates raw tick streams and identifies structural compression and expansion sequences. You will learn to view the market as a continuous, mathematically sound physical structure, not a series of random indicator line crossings.
- **Step Button:** `[ Commit to Scientific Price Structure ]`

---

### Step 3: Deploying the Shadow Simulation Engine
- **Visual Asset:** A mock-up of the $1,194 Demo Trading Dashboard with a pulsing status badge: "Learning Mode Active".
- **Screen Title:** **Principle 3: Factual and Disciplined Validation**
- **Copy:**
  We believe consistency is trained, not guessed. When you enter the terminal, you will be initialized with a standard virtual demo account containing exactly **$1,194 USD**.

  This capital is used to test and refine your models. Every trade you simulated in demo mode is audited by our independent **Judge Brain**. It records your successes and failures, writes detailed learning records, and updates your strategy consistency score. You are here to learn and validate before you trade.
- **Step Button:** `[ Initialize My Validation Balance ($1,194.00) ]`
