## 1. The Narrative Shift: Intelligence Maturity vs Numerical Limits

Traditionally, SaaS platforms gate their pricing tiers purely on arbitrary numeric limitations (e.g., "3 symbols versus 10 symbols"). While TradeYar AI retains these technical limits under the hood to satisfy system load constraints, our outward brand communication shifts the focus to **Intelligence Maturity**.

Traders do not subscribe to access more charts; they subscribe to unlock deeper layers of **Cognitive Reasoning, Multi-Horizon Verification, and Independent Auditing**.

```
[ Research Access ($0) ] ───► [ Daily Intelligence ($29) ] ───► [ Professional Reasoning ($79) ] ───► [ Institutional Intelligence ($299) ]
```

## 3. High-Conversion Pricing FAQs

### Q: Why isn't there a "Live Trading" button on any tier?
**A:** In absolute compliance with international APES-FIN standards, TradeYar AI is a read-only analytical platform. We believe the key to trading success is mathematical validation and cognitive conditioning, not automated retail execution. Your capital remains safe in your broker account, completely isolated from algorithmic risks.

### Q: What is the significance of the $1,194 balance in Demo Trading?
**A:** TradeYar AI's validation system simulates a standardized, high-discipline starting capital of exactly **$1,194 USD** for demo accounts. This specific amount represents our standard baseline for evaluating risk management performance. It allows our Cognitive Learning models to audit and score traders fairly, using standard risk limits (e.g., 10% daily risk cap).
