# Monetization Strategy
*TradeYar AI — Strategic Architecture & Capability Audit*

---

## 1. Passive Compliance Monetization Philosophy

To strictly adhere to **APES-FIN non-trading read-only guidelines**, TradeYar AI must never monetize active trade signals or automate financial transactions. Instead, its revenue model is built entirely around **educational insights, market intelligence, quantitative reasoning, and personal trading logs**.

---

## 2. Product Line Tier Architecture

### Product 1 — Free Tier (User Acquisition)
* **Target Audience**: Retail hobbyists, financial students, and general market followers.
* **Pricing**: $0 / Month.
* **Core Capabilities**:
  - **Market Observation**: Access to live, read-only price-action sequence visualizations for standard symbols (e.g. EURUSD).
  - **Daily Reports**: General PDF/Web summary of overnight market events and pattern occurrences.
  - **Educational Insights**: Qualitative explanations of detected structures to help users learn clean price-action concepts.

---

### Product 2 — Premium Tier (Core Subscriber)
* **Target Audience**: Aspiring traders seeking quantitative validation for their manual trading ideas.
* **Pricing**: $29 / Month.
* **Core Capabilities**:
  - **AI Research Assistant**: Access to the bilingual chat interface to query market memories.
  - **Deep Analysis**: Unlimited similarity queries for custom-drawn price-action signatures.
  - **Memory System Access**: Access to historical Pattern and Concept memories.
  - **Historical Reasoning**: Ability to ask "Why were you wrong?" or "What did you learn?" on any historical market replay session.

---

### Product 3 — Professional Tier (Active Trader)
* **Target Audience**: Active prop-firm and professional retail traders seeking strict performance analytics.
* **Pricing**: $79 / Month.
* **Core Capabilities**:
  - **Shadow Trading Portfolio**: Real-time virtual accounts with customizable leverage and spreads.
  - **Performance Analysis**: Deep adverse/favorable excursion metrics (MAE/MFE) plotted automatically.
  - **AI Trading Journal**: The Judge Brain automatically audits every virtual trade, labeling trades as "Earned Success", "Lucky Win", or "Structural Failure".
  - **Risk Intelligence**: Real-time warnings regarding confidence-inflation and confirmation bias in the user's manual hypotheses.

---

### Product 4 — B2B Tier (Institutional / Community)
* **Target Audience**: Trading academies, financial brokers, educational Discord/Telegram communities, and professional quantitative teams.
* **Pricing**: Custom contract (e.g. starting at $499 / Month).
* **Core Capabilities**:
  - **White-Label Web Portals**: Communities can host the System Validation Center under their own brand.
  - **API Gateway Access**: Programmatic read-only queries for historical patterns and concept structures.
  - **Team Workspaces**: Shared virtual portfolios where multiple student traders can log virtual shadow trades and have them audited by the same Judge Brain.
