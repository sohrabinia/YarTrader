# Outcome Evaluation Engine

## 1. Overview
After future market data becomes available, completed shadow trades are evaluated to measure:
- Profit/Loss
- Maximum Favorable Movement (MFE)
- Maximum Adverse Movement (MAE)
- Holding duration
- Entry and Exit quality
- Reason and Hypothesis validity

---

## 2. Decision Quality vs. Profit
A profitable trade is not automatically considered correct. Correct reasoning and pattern validity are prioritized over lucky trade outcomes.
