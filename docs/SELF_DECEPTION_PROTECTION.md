## 2. Hardened Safeguards
- **Confirmation Bias**: Failures, losses, and rejected hypotheses are permanently preserved as learning materials.
- **Look-Ahead & Snooping**: The future is strictly hidden from the simulation brain until evaluation occurs.
- **Confidence Inflation**: Calibrated confidence scores require increased sample size verification and low error rates, or they automatically decrease.
- **Immutability**: Simulated trade records, once locked, are completely immutable.
