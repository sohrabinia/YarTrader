# USER GUIDE
**TradeYar AI Speculator's Dashboard Manual**

Welcome to TradeYar AI! Follow this guide to onboard and customize your financial market intelligence workspace.

---

## 1. Onboarding & Account Registration
1. Visit the TradeYar homepage (`/`).
2. Click the **Register** button in the header navigation or the home card.
3. Enter your email/username and secure password (minimum 6 characters).
4. Choose your initial subscription level (FREE, PRO, or PREMIUM).
5. Upon registration, you will receive a simulated **Welcome email** confirming activation.

---

## 2. Secure Account Login & Recovery
- **Login**: Navigate to `/login` or select the Login tab, fill in your credentials, and submit. The server issues a cryptographically secure token cookie (`tradeyar_session`) keeping you logged in.
- **Password Recovery**:
  1. Click **Recover Password**.
  2. Input your registered email.
  3. A 6-digit numeric recovery code will be dispatched to your email address (and represented in the UI for simulation-readiness).
  4. Fill in the recovery code and your desired new password, then submit to regain account access.

---

## 3. Customizing Your Workspace Dashboard
- **Market Watchlist**: Keep your favorite symbols near your eyes! Use the Watchlist component to select and pin `XAUUSD`, `EURUSD`, or `GBPUSD`. Pinned symbols initiate active, read-only analytics polling.
- **AI Support Assistant**: Chat directly with the TradeYar AI Support Bot! Ask about platform pricing, market terms, or risk factors.
  *Note: Free accounts are protected by a quota limit of 10 requests per day. Upgrade your account to increase resource availability.*

---

## 4. Upgrading Subscriptions (Simulation-Ready Payments)
1. Select the **My Dashboard** tab.
2. Under the **Subscription Upgrades & Payments** section, select:
   - **PRO** ($29.99/mo)
   - **PREMIUM** ($99.99/mo)
3. Click the **Simulated Crypto Gateway Pay** payment button.
4. The system automatically processes the mock crypto invoice, completes accounting logs, updates your role permissions, fires a billing invoice email, and alerts you via Telegram!
